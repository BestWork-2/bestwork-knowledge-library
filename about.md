## Purpose

This library preserves and organizes BestWork articles, case studies, and practical perspectives so they can be read, searched, cited, and maintained on the open web.

## Provenance

The documents were converted from an internal library of PDF, Apple Pages, and text-clipping files. Document pages identify the original source filename and format. Conversion preserves the substantive text while presenting it in accessible HTML.

## Editorial status

The collection includes material written across multiple years. Publication in this archive does not necessarily mean that every claim, link, example, or legal reference has been updated. Material involving hiring, assessments, law, or psychometrics should be reviewed against current professional and legal standards before operational use.

One scanned document required optical character recognition and may contain minor transcription errors. Corrections are welcome through the repository's issue tracker.

## Rights

Unless a document states otherwise, its contents are copyrighted by BestWork and/or the identified author. Public access does not grant permission to republish or commercially reuse the material.
