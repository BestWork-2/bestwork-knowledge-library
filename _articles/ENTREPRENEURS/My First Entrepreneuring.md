## Page 1

My first memory of entrepreneuring is of an event when I was about 9 or 10

years old, when I couldn’t have even pronounced the term. I had

discovered a wonderful water pistol at Kress 5 & 10¢ store…an almost

fluorescent orange with the largest capacity I had ever seen. It was a water

pistol that would leave my enemies dripping with envy.

Unfortunately, it was $1.29, and although I occasionally was given a quarter

to spend however I wanted, at that point in my life, it was a financial chasm

with me on the wrong side. Of course as more prudent child would have

seen that by carefully saving the quarter and other coins that might come

my way over the next several months, I would eventually be able to accrue

the necessary funds. For me this was out of the question. Summer was

almost upon us. I needed to be armed and ready for the season.

That was when that first entrepreneurial thought reared its creative head. I

had received a Viewmaster projector for Christmas with a whole set of

disks. I would do a show…my own movie show, and I would charge 10¢

admission. The glitch here is that I am an only child, and we lived out in the

country. That meant that after Mom and Dad bought their tickets, I would

still be well short of summer fun and playground domination. But wait! I had

a bed full of stuffed animals who would love to attend the show. I am sure

my Dad held back his smile as I filled my theatre with the crowd of panda

bears, a stuffed dog and a chimpanzee that held my pj’s on sleepaways.

These ten movie goers plus my parents made for a cool profit of $1.20 plus

my quarter. The water pistol was mine!!
