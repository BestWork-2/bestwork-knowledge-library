## Page 1

BestWork DATA’s Choice of Big Five vs. Big Six

The HEXACO model of personality, the Big Six, is based on the lexical

studies conducted in multiple languages, which revealed a “6th” factor

beyond the Big 5. That was termed Honesty-Humility, with the facets of

Sincerity, Fairness, Greed Avoidance and Modesty. It is important to

differentiate the goals of the HEXACO research and its findings from the

goals of BestWork DATA. The first was an academic and scientific pursuit

to discover a more complete model for personality. The goal of BestWork

DATA was take a proven psychological model and extract the facets of

hard-wired personality traits that directly impact job behaviors across a

wide range of job categories. Then BestWork DATA uses top level

psychometric science with a few advances to create an instrument and an

experience that accurately measures those traits. While a case can be

made for all facets having an impact, the objective was to identify those

that are accurately measurable and discrete so as to allow a clear

connection with actionable decisions.

An example of a Big 5 facet that was omitted from the BestWork DATA

construct is Aesthetic Appreciation, a facet of Openness to New

Experiences. This is clearly important to artists and designers, but is a

minor consideration for the majority of jobs. Therefore it was not included.

The emphasis was on the factors critical to sales, management, production,

administration, marketing and other common business roles.

Similarly, the factor of Honesty -Humility, while interesting, is prey to a world of influential forces and environmental elements that create an unacceptable variability. There is also a major Values component in it, which can be surveyed but not accurately measured.

BestWork DATA is focused on providing decision-making knowledge, using

the information derived from the DATA acquired through the participant’s

interaction with the BestWork DATA experience. Part of the value provided

is the removal of extraneous information so as to focus the client’s

attention on the key points affecting their decisions.
