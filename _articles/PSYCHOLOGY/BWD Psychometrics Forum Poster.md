## Page 1

Introduction• Research supporting the use of personality and intelligence testing to inform personnel decisions is robust.• Organizations that persist without assessments or employ inferior methods struggle to attract, hire, train, develop, and retain employees. • Additionally, the tests that are commonly used have not substantially evolved since their inception (Nikolaou & Oostrom, 2015).• An extensive 3-year study of more than 5,000 hiring managers revealed that only 19% of new hires went on to be successful in the opinion of the hiring companies (Leadership IQ).• This research focused on integrating reporting from a scientifically derived questionnaire into multiple stages of business decisions within a B2B Service Company thus quantifiably improving sales, turnover, and productivity.Methodology• Phase 1 - B2B Service Company made business decisions without the BestWork DATA (BWD). • Phase 2 - introduced BWD in June of 2012 which began with an inventory of the sales team. – BWD consists of a simple 25 minute online experience and measures the hard-wired traits and abilities that determine how a person thinks, learns and behaves. • Phase 3 - full integration of BWD into hiring, onboarding, and training processes by 2014. • Predictive validity was established by measuring key factors over a period of five years: sales, growth, attrition.

Mission StatementTo touch the lives of hundreds of millions of people so that they will see themselves through their strengths instead of their weaknesses and so that they can better understand other people. Our products must: honor each person, offer a positive emotional experience and provide actionable information.

Results• Prior to introduction of the BWD experience, the organization selected sales team members to train and promote and could not discern why some were successful while others were not. • After testing was introduced in Phases 2 and 3 all performance benchmarks improved significantly and the organization continues to rely on DATA for all business decisions.• New Sales Growth: $3.4M to $15.6M over 6 years (459% Total Growth and 28.9% CAGR) •32%, 37% and 38% growth the 1st 3 years, consistent low 20%'s • Sales Team Size: 26 to 59 employees over the same period (227% Growth in Team, 202% Growth in Productivity)• Sales Team Turnover: 31% to 7% over the same period (24% Reduction in Turnover)•10% turnover by the 2nd year and remained consistent (+/- 2%) Research Contributions• This research supports the use of BWD as a critical tool throughout the employee lifecycle; contributing to an organization’s ability to effectively select, onboard, promote, retain the right person for the job. • Similar results have been demonstrated across a broad spectrum of industries and jobs. • The conclusion is that BWD can be used in any setting to gain outcomes that are favorable to both the employee and the organization. • Integration of the BWD experience into business processes increases efficiency and reduces costs.• The implications for organizational performance are that better data contributes to better decisions and yields better performance.
