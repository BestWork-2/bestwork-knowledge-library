## Page 1

Every person has hard-wired strengths and abilities that they never lose.

Those strengths are aways there…regardless of their level of education.

Regardless of any disabilities…regardless of sex, race or religion.

Each and every person has these wonderful strengths and abilities.

In some situations, those strengths are advantages. In others, they are

obstacles. Being seven feet tall is an advantage on a basketball court. In a coal mine, it is an obstacle. Unfortunately, in most situations in life, how well those strengths fit is not nearly as clear and obvious as height in sports and mining.

These strengths and abilities are the foundation of every success they will ever

have. They are also the basis of every failure. Without knowing exactly what

these strengths and abilities are, they will never be able to reach their full

potential. Much of life will be left to chance. Understanding these strengths and abilities enables anyone to make more effective choices and to avoid many difficulties.
