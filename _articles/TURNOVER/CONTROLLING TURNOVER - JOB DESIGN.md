Controlling Turnover - Job Design

Job Design

Most jobs have been designed with little or no consideration of hard-wired behavioral traits and cognitive abilities. Consequently the result is what can be called tall-short jobs. The individual is asked to deliver two opposite types of job behavior. Personality traits and cognitive abilities are distributed across the population. BestWork measures each trait and ability for an individual, locating where each is in relation to the general population. Each trait and ability has extremes on each end of the bell curve.

Example A

A management position was created within a large, well run organization. There were two primary responsibilities for this manager : •The first was to conduct highly detailed analysis of data sets tracking the operation of several key business divisions.

•The second was to respond to unexpected events within those divisions.

The analysis that was the core of the first responsibility requires high attention to details, which is a hard-wired personality trait. The ability to respond to unplanned events is found on the opposite end of that personality trait scale. A person could handle the response requirement if they were wired to do so but then their ability to handle details diminishes. The company had created a tall-short job. BestWork enabled the company to redesign the job requirements into two lower level roles that were easily filled.

Example B

A professional services firm had a persistent problem in retaining a key role in their marketing department. The position was well paid with excellent benefits, but no matter how rich was the compensation, no one stayed beyond a few months. The job involved two quite different things.

•The person managed a creative team of artists, directing their work and assigning projects.

•The person was also responsible for ensuring that partners within the firm has signed off on all marketing projects. This included signing off on any changes to rhe projects, of which there were many. These partners had limited time, limited patience and limited interested in approving the changes and the costs they incurred.

Managing creative talent requires a high level of sensitivity as the artists themselves are highly sensitive. Obtaining signatures from high level partners required a bulldog mentality and an ironclad sensitivity. The company had combined two jobs into one, and that one was a tall-short job. Using BestWork, the job was split into two parts, easily filled and with the combined compensation almost the same as the original tall-short one.
