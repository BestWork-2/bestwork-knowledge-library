How Behavioral DATA Transforms Screening and Selection

The ability to measure a candidate’s hard-wired personality traits and

cognitive abilities completely transforms screening and selection

processes. All jobs have certain job behaviors that are critical to

performing that job successfully. BestWork can automatically screen out

candidates that lack the critical factors. This collapses the time in the

selection process. All of the remaining candidates can do the job, and

BestWork’s full range of behavior information can help determine which

ones can do it best.

When there are multiple jobs to consider, BestWork can identify which

ones are the best match for the candidate’s strengths and abilities. Some

project of this type are : Ski Resorts This company owned and operated a collection of ski resorts in North America. In a typical year, they received 16,000 applications for the 62 jobs in the resorts. A team of HR personnel looked at resumés and interviewed the candidates to place them in the jobs. This was incredibly time- consuming and only marginally successful. We designed a system that captured the candidates’ behavioral DATA at the same time the application was submitted. That DATA was matched with the 62 jobs and the candidates were automatically sorted into the jobs they fit. The interview then became a simple discussion about which of the fitting jobs they preferred.

BestWork’s behavioral DATA enables remarkable differentiation between

jobs. An excellent example of this was the selection of ski lift operators for

the base lifts and the upper mountain lifts. All of the lift operator

candidates shared the same consistency with safety rules and procedures.

The important difference was how they interacted with the guests. Base

operators were friendly and outgoing, which was exactly what the guests

expected as a welcome to their skiing day. Once they were on the

mountain, friendliness and interaction were not necessary and they only

wanted efficiency. This is possible for all jobs within a company. The

ultimate hiring decisions are still made by people but the principal

variables that are the cause of most bad hires are largely eliminated.

Healthcare Recruiter

This company specialized in recruiting for twelve healthcare jobs ranging

from physician’s assistant to simple caregiver roles. They typically received

a thousand applications each month. Four of the jobs were more complex,

requiring special training. The majority of their candidates were responding

to the other eight jobs which could easily be learned if the job fit was

good. Most of these candidates used mobile phones, so BestWork

created a shorter 15 minute version of the experience. Previously the

recruiters relied on on a series of questionnaires, resumés and interviews

to select out their candidates. The process was complex and time-

consuming, depending on multiple subjective opinions on the various sets

of information. BestWork replaced that process, automatically screening

out the candidates that did not fit any of the jobs, and then sorting the

others into the jobs they did fit. Now the interviews were always with

candidates that could do the job. The decisions were quicker and needed

far less manpower.

A group working in Africa received a grant to train 200 students for cyber

security jobs. This was a pilot program with much larger projects planned

for the future, with the caveat that the pilot was successful. Over 400

students registered for the first cohort. The challenge was to select those

students who were most likely to be successful in the training programs.

BestWork reviewed the training program, identifying two critical

requirements for success : •Fast speed of learning and processing information - The classes involved highly complex situations to be mastered in a fairly short time period.

•Ability to work with extremely detailed information - Cyber

security depends on handling a myriad of details accurately.

BestWork measured these factors for each student, sorting them into four

categories : Excellent (fast learning & strong details),Good (fast learning &

good details or strong details & faster than average learning), Acceptable

(faster than average learning & better than average details) and Not

Acceptable (Below average learning &/or below average details).

The first cohort moved quickly through the training, scoring a 96%

completion of the first set of training. They continued to progress ahead of

expectations.

High Volume Recruiting

This fast-paced recruiting company received about 1,000 applications

weekly from offshore locations, applying for 40 different jobs. Verification

of English fluency and available computer technology cut the number to

about 500 candidates each week. A team of people reviewed resumés and

interviewed that group, reducing it to about 250 after several days. This

bottleneck limited the number of applicants that could be processed.

BestWork mapped the critical job behaviors for each of the jobs. The

BestWork system was inserted into the process at the 500 applicant point.

Candidates who lacked the critical job factors were automatically

screened out. Those that moved forward were automatically sorted into
