Sales Thoughts #1

Can each member of your sales team close sales successfully?

Working with over a hundred sales teams, we found that : 25% of the best sales teams were completely unable to sell the products or services offered by their company. This was the best percentage. A Fortune 100 had 44% of their principal sales team incapable of selling the solutions being offered.

These salespeople were intelligent and personable professionals, trained in

sales skills, and most were working hard at their jobs. They lacked the hard-

wired strengths and abilities to perform the skills needed for that particular

type of sale.

BestWork measures those strengths and abilities, and those measurements

can be compared with any behaviors for any job. With that information,

anyone can see if an individual can successfully perform any job, and how to

support them most effectively in that job. BestWork is not an assessment

comparing people to some profile or template. BestWork simply answers

the question of “Can they do this job?”

The question for you is “At what point do you want to know the answer?”

Identifying the Critical Job Behaviors in the Sale

There are many types of sales. There are simple product sales and complex

solution sales. There are sales that require a team of specialized

professionals, and others in which one person must do it all. Regardless of

the differences and the variety of sales, they all share three key elements : A problem or objective must be identified.

A solution must be presented.

The prospect must be persuaded to make a buying decision.

The other key factor that BestWork shows is the salesperson’s ability to handle a complex or solution sale as opposed to a straightforward product or services sale.

Example C illustrates the idea range for selling complex or solution sales. The

complexity and multiple variables that are generally the core of a solution sale

require the ability to process information quickly. Each opportunity is different, and the salesperson must adapt their approach and presentation to the situation.

Example C

The sale of products and standard services tends to fall into a pattern or sales

process that is repeatable. In fact, once the right sales approach is discovered, the best plan is to simply repeat it. Ironically, the best solution salespeople find it difficult to stick with a plan like that, even though it has proven to be successful.

Complex sales often present another challenge that requires key strengths and

abilities and that is controlling the sales process. More assertive salespeople work at moving the sales process along through multiple decision makers and various checkpoints. They tend to stay in touch along the way with calls, emails, updates and additional information. Less assertive salespeople let the process unfold as it will, often losing momentum and support on the buyer’ side. Example D illustrates how BestWork alerts the sales manager to this danger.
