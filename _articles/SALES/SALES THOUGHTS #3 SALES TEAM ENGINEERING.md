Sales Thoughts #3

Understanding the overall strengths of your sales team?

Effective sales management is a mix of selecting, training and coaching individuals and organizing them into a team. The collective strengths of the team must support the strategic goals of the enterprise. This can be difficult to discern because the focus is continually switching among the various individual salespeople. BestWork does this easily, presenting the DATA that shows the overall strengths of the sales team in multiple formats that showcase various perspectives of those strengths.

The chart shown below is a 48 person sales team in a highly competitive industry, selling a complex product offering multiple solutions that typically involved several levels of decisions. This is an inventory of the strengths of that sales team produced by BestWork. These strengths tend to remain the same regardless of training, coaching or motivational incentives. They are like the height of a basketball player in the NBA. If they are short, they remain short, and no matter what training or coaching is used, it must deal with that fact. The behavioral DATA shows the performance issues that are unlikely to change. This enables the company to adjust their expectations and more importantly, to identify the the salespeople that can be effective and the strategies that can be successful with them.
