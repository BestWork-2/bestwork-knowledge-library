The Human Factor in Cyber Security

The primary efforts to strengthen cyber security have focused on two areas : Technology and Protocols. While these are clearly vital components of any cyber security strategy, time and again the greatest vulnerability has proven to be the human factor. The fatal glitch in the technology occurred because people overlooked something or simply made a mistake. The protocol was breached because a person bent a rule; took a shortcut; or intentionally attacked the system. Even when this has been recognized, the common response has been to simply intensify training or tighten the technology.

Human beings have hard-wired personality traits and cognitive abilities which

are foundational to their thinking, learning and behavior. These are also

foundational to how they interact with cyber security systems. Measuring these

traits and abilities provides behavioral DATA that can effectively map the human

vulnerabilities that have the potential to disrupt a cyber security system.

IMPORTANT NOTE : The vulnerabilities can be indexed in relative terms. A high vulnerability does not mean that a person will definitely do anything to breach or compromise a cyber system. It means that there is a greater likelihood of that happening. A simple metaphor is that of a group of people spending the day at the beach. It is possible to identify which person is most likely to get sunburned, but it does not mean that they will get sunburned. Indexing vulnerability gives management reference points for positioning people in such a way as to minimize risk.

The Vulnerabilities & Security Factors

Information Processing Speed

- Vulnerability : Slower processing speed does not recognize the future

consequences of actions in the present.

- Security : Faster processing speed enables projecting the probable

consequences of actions in the present, thereby recognizing a threat.

Calling Out Problems

- Vulnerability : Hesitancy to call out a problem or to ask the hard questions

avoiding debate or challenge opens weak points.

- Security : The willingness to call out a problem or challenge a potential threat

adds to security.

Rules & Procedures

- Vulnerability : Easy willingness to make exceptions to rules and procedures

creates high vulnerability.

- Security : Strict consistency and compliance with rules and procedures

strengthens defense.

Attention to Details

- Vulnerability : Inability to handle details accurately causes password, user

names and email errors. Homemade solutions like writing passwords down

invite disaster.

- Security : Strong attention to details supports strong passwords/usernames

and strengthens defense.

Emotional Sensitivity

- Vulnerability : Can have a certain naiveté and openness to unfamiliar messages

making them vulnerable to scams.

- Security : Generally pessimistic attitude causes them to question everything

and avoid scams.

Need to Interact with Others

- Security : Tendency to be reserved and private reduces their interaction with

possible security threats.

- Vulnerability : Their warmth and friendliness creates potential openings for

fraudulent engagements.

Competitive Nature

- Vulnerability : Strong desire to win and be “right” can play into certain types of

scams.

- Vulnerability : Willingness and desire to help others creates vulnerability to

certain types of scams.

Practical Strategies

A 25 minute online experience accurately and reliably measures these traits and

abilities. The behavioral DATA from this experience generates a vulnerability

index for that individual. Those with high vulnerability can be directed to area

with minimal security risks, and those with low vulnerability can be placed in

areas with greater risks. The DATA can also generate a collective index for a

work group, a department or any collective set of employees.

The behavioral DATA can be enhanced with the addition of specialized

supplemental surveys focused on an individual’s attitude regarding cyber

security and their personal experiences with security protocols and practices.

The combination of the behavioral DATA and the information from the surveys

provides valuable insights into the human factors that can threaten cyber

security of any enterprise.
