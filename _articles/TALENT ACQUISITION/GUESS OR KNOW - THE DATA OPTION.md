## Page 1

Guess or Know - The DATA Option with the WHAT IF Information

The News…

Today it is possible to easily and accurately measure how people think,

learn and behave. This information is called DATA.

So what?

It means that if you are hiring a salesperson, DATA will tell you IF the

person can sell before you spend time interviewing them.

If you are promoting someone to a management or supervisory position,

DATA will tell you IF they are able to direct the performance of others

before they find themselves in the wrong role.

If you are planning to train a group of employees, DATA will tell you IF the

training will make a difference in the performance of each person.

Essentially, you eliminate bad hires. You promote the right person into the

right role for them. You do not waste money training the wrong people.

It can’t be that simple…

It is based on a disruptive innovation that changes the way we understand

human performance. It is like an MRI, showing us the foundation of all

human behavior.

It must be hard to understand.

Actually, that is one of the disruptions. It can easily be understood by

anyone, without any special training or interpretation by experts.

How is that possible? I don’t know anything about psychology.

Instead of complex graphs filled with amazing information that is

annoyingly difficult to connect with the daily questions in your business,

you have sentences in plain language that describe if the individual can

deliver the key job behaviors needed in a specific position and how they

will do it if they can.
