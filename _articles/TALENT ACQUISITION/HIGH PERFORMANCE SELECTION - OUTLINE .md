High Performance Selection Process - Outline

This process is effective for any position. It accelerates the selection of talent by eliminating poor candidates that would be bad hires. It focuses time and attention on hiring talent that not only can do the job, but that fits into the existing workforce. This process helps the employer and the new employee become productive much faster and mesh with the existing team easier.
