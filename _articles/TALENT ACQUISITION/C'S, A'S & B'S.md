## Page 1

Avoid the C’s, Love the A’s…but Grab the B’s

Recruiting with DATA

Every company wants to recruit top performers…definitely a cool idea.

Unfortunately, that simple strategy contains an inherent glitch that often

hinders rather than helps in finding talent. To explain, essentially there are

three categories of employees: •A’s - A’s set records, perform beyond expectations, require little management, and the the dream hire of every recruiter.

•B’s - B’s perform adequately and meet expectations, although

they do require some level of management time and energy.

•C’s - C’s do not perform acceptably despite significant efforts by

management nor with additional coaching or training.

The primary and most effective way of using BestWork is to screen out C’s.

Newer generations of assessments can easily and accurately do this

because their DATA is more precise as to specific job behaviors. Older

instruments lumped everything together and compared the whole to a

profile or benchmark derived from a sample of A performers. It was clearly

aimed at identifying more A’s. While this seemed to make sense, it had

three flaws: 1.C’s still got in from time to time, and even one C costs a fortune in time, energy and money.

2.The profile’s focus on A’s sometimes caused talented B’s to be

excluded. In other words, an unsatisfactory score on some traits,

offset the exceptional strengths in some more critical traits.

3.Hiring decisions tended to hinge on the percentage match to the

profile. Once the hire was made, the percentage score was

useless for development, training or management. Even when

these types of products offered additional reports, the text was

usually so generalized or contradictory as to offer little in the way

of actionable information.
