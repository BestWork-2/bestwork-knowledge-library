The Super Differentiator for LLM Applications

There is no doubt that AI offers a myriad of possibilities for the business

world. The role of human beings will evolve dramatically and the objectives

of the roles will be much more de fined. People will remain the most

important variable in the operation of those businesses that will continue

to employ people in concert with AI. The reduction of the variability of the

performance of those people will be a key factor in the success of almost

every enterprise with people. The level of behavioral DATA that is available

now does more than reduce the variability…it is a super differentiator for

LLM applications.

Behavioral DATA is accurately and reliably provided by two 7th Generation

psychometric instruments, using a 25 minute online experience. That DATA

is foundational to all thinking, learning and behaving. This DATA has been

proven with thousands of applications for the talent acquisition and talent

management of individuals. AI has the ability to recognize the relationships

within work groups. AI can see the directional deltas between the

behavioral strengths of two people and e ffectively map the rules of

engagement that will optimize communication between them and avoid

the potential glitches that plague communication effectiveness in every

organization. AI can also incorporate key correlations among those

strengths, noting how they may affect communications.

While the possibilities AI brings to a two person relationship are

impressive, increasing the number of people unleashing the true power of

AI. It analyzes all of deltas, between individuals, between an individual and

the collective group, between subgroups, and it factors in operational

issues such as position in the organization and current objectives of the

group.

LLM applications can be tasked to evaluate the group’s collective

strengths with regard to their overall mission. It can recommend additions

to the group or changes within the group. It can recommend leadership

initiatives and propose rules of engagement for the group. These may vary

as the mission of the group changes. These may include problem solving,

brainstorming new ideas, debating different strategies and more.

AI talks to people. With behavioral DATA, AI can talk to individuals.

The capability to obtain the DATA exists now. The proposal is to develop a

commercial LLM application that can provide these group analytics.
