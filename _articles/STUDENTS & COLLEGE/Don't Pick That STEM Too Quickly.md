## Page 1

It is not enough to identify the jobs of the future and direct students to them.

STEM is a not a magical place with all roads leading to desirable jobs. All

students are not right for all jobs. Human beings have hard-wired strengths and

abilities that remain with them throughout their lives. These strengths are the

foundation of all of their successes, and they are the cause of most of their

failures. Which one of these happens is largely dependent on how well each

person’s strengths match up with what is needed to perform the job successfully.

Today it is easy to identify and measure these strengths and abilities, and then

they can be compared to various types of jobs to see which ones fit and which

ones do not fit. All students fit some jobs, but all students do not fit all jobs. The reality is that STEM-jobs are largely dependent on several key strengths.

Science - Half of all students will not be happy in science-related jobs.

Technology - Half of all students will not prosper in technology jobs.

Engineering - More than half of all students will not be good engineers.

Math - Half of all students will not excel in math-related jobs.
