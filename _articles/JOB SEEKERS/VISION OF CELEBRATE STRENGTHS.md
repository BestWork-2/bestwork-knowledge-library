The Vision of Celebrate Strengths

The vision of Celebrate Strengths is much more than creating and

marketing wonderful tools for the business world. It begins in the Mission

Statement: To touch the lives of hundreds of millions of people so that they see themselves through their strengths instead of their weaknesses, and so that they better understand others.

Even without the economic challenges in today’s world, people struggle to

understand their true strengths. They doubt themselves, and in doubting,

make poor choices and miss opportunities. Over half of all doctors or

lawyers wish they were in a different profession. More than half of all

people dislike their current job or wish they were in another one. They lack

the information to connect their strengths with opportunities in business.

People struggle to communicate with each other because they cannot

clearly appreciate the other person’s point of view.

Job seekers are continually misled with a host of outdated and archaic

assessments, and career counselors fumble around with generalized

advice that has no real connection to their clients. In schools, high school

juniors and seniors are offered antiquated tools to guide their decisions on

careers and college majors. That is a key reason that almost half of those

that enter college never finish. It is the main reason that the average

student changes majors more than twice, and it is why six years to

complete what used to be a four year degree is common.

DATA from serious psychometric instruments offers the true guidance and

direction that all of these people seek. BestWork, JobThoughts, Students &

College and Try On Jobs were created to translate that DATA into practical

tools for ordinary people. These tools have the power to change the lives of

people. They can offer answers that anyone can understand.
