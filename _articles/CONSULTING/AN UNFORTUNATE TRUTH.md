BestWork & Consultants - An Unfortunate Truth

Having been a successful management consultant for many years and

having advised hundreds of other consultants on how to grow their

businesses, I must expose an unfortunately real and inconvenient truth

about consultants and BestWork. There are two distinctly different

approaches that consultants use with BestWork, and each has a

completely different result in virtually every instance.

Approach A: Approach A is the rarest, which is puzzling since it is the

approach that is most successful. Consultants using Approach A see the

BestWork simply as a tool that has the ability to engage a prospect at the

most intimate level of their business, suggesting the ability to answer the

performance questions the prospects asks themselves every day. They

begin each potential engagement with the “Would it make sense to

inventory the strengths of your team so that we can see more specifically

what the issues may be and the easiest way to resolve them?” In

presenting the BestWork inventory, that consultant immediately becomes a

Trusted Advisor, knowing more about the employees than the prospect.

The consultant has skipped the sales role, moving directly to “May I make a

recommendation?” The consultant’s knowledge, experience and whatever

methods or models they favor now easily flow into the engagement.

BestWork allows for a deeper understanding of the enterprise, opening the

door for multiple added engagements. The consultant is clearly the STAR

of the show, not BestWork. It is the artist, not the brush, that creates the art.
