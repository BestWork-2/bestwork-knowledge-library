Team Engineering Capabilities with BestWork

One of the most powerful capabilities in the BestWork System is

Team Engineering. This allows a client to view the distribution of

strengths and abilities within any team or workgroup. The

similarities and gaps among the individuals offer an easy to

understand visual map of communication challenges within the

team. Similarly, the team engineering function can be used to direct

effective collaboration among the team members. Identifying the

manager or any individual within the team chart helps to recognize

any deltas (differences) that may present challenges. This can be

the key to optimizing the performance of the overall team.

BestWork offers several specialized applications for team

engineering : •Executive Team Strengths Inventory - designed for executive teams •Team Interaction Map - ideal for any workgroup •Sales Team Strengths Inventory - designed for sales teams •Communication Characteristics Map - focuses on communication within a workgroup •Collaboration Capabilities Map - focused on collaboration within a workgroup NOTE & CAUTION : While the team engineering charts are wonderful tools for working with teams, the charts are not designed for hiring or selection applications. In too many cases where a client used the team engineering chart for a single job candidate, the result was a poor hiring decision. These charts are designed to facilitate quick and accurate

decisions, only focusing on three critical factors. Team engineering

charts however, include as many as 19 different factors, each of

which affects job performance to a different degree. Some of those

factors are easily managed or supported, but others are not. In a

sense, the Quick Screen Charts are designed to screen out

candidates with those problems before they ever reach the point of

looking at the other factors. When all of the factors are visible,

human nature tends to try to balance one with the other, believing

one good one can compensate for a bad one. Unfortunately, that is

not how it really works. While clients have been cautioned about

this, it still persisted. BestWork’s goal is to have our clients optimize

their workforce, for both the company and the employees. We are

continually refining our products to achieve that goal. The team

engineering reports are still available, and in fact, new ones have

been added. The application now requires two or more individuals

to produce the chart. It will no longer produce a chart for one

person. It may be useful to produce a report for a candidate with

their proposed manager. This makes the candidate’s results

available with enhanced information of how their strengths match

the manager’s strengths.

Our mission is to provide you with accurate information that you can

rely on for people decisions. Part of that mission is to design the

system so as to encourage effective use of that information and to

minimize ways to misread it. BestWork will soon be expanding the

applications you have now with a far easier to use interface and

solutions that include talent acquisition and talent management. We

appreciate your confidence in us.
