Call Center Case Study Story

The following case study illustrates how the Call Center MRI™ information drives tactical and strategic decisions that can dramatically impact longstanding performance issues. Acme is a real company, a division of a Fortune 500 corporation, but the name has been changed.

Acme is an insurance company, providing personal coverage to a wide range of customers.

Traditionally, this call center team answered inbound calls from customers inquiring about various issues regarding their coverage. Agents accessed the customer’s account, and then reported that information to the customer, with whatever explanation was necessary. If the situation was unusual or involved any decisions on the part of the agent, the call was tranferred to a supervisor. This was a fairly routine process, and excepting the problem of attrition, the call center performed adequately.

ACME’S CHALLENGES

Acme is currently seeking to grow revenue through its most obvious resource, its existing customer base. Each day, hundreds of customers call into the call center asking questions about their policies. This seemed to be an opportunity for the agents to engage these customers by suggesting options to enhance their existing coverage. The various options would be indicated by the customer’s information screen, and the agent would select the best one to recommend to the customer.

While this new strategy is conceptually sound, its practical execution is completely dependent upon job capabilities that are very different from the ones needed for the original strategy. An inventory of the existing competencies within the current call center team must be taken in order to: •Verify the viability of the new strategy using the existing team •Determine an effective training program for the existing team •Develop a tactical plan to deploy the existing resources in the most effective manner •Develop a strategic recruiting model for the new strategy.

Summary

The information provided by the Call Center MRI™ Study enabled Acme to understand why talented and hardworking agents had been unable to realize the full marketplace potential of their unique product and service offerings. While there was not a quick solution at hand, there was now a clear course of action, both strategically and tactically. Through a combination of changes in training and responsibilities plus a strategic redeployment of certain agents, it was possible to move forward with the new strategy, while recruiting for agents more capable of filling the new roles.

While there is no magic wand for call center success, the Call Center MRI™ does provide unprecedented information on the capabilities of any group of agents. It does not presume to tell you if they are good or bad, but whether your team has the capabilities to successfully drive the business strategy and processes of your company or whether those strategies are viable with your existing call center team. The Call Center MRI™ gives call center managers the information they need to immediately determine their team’s strengths and weaknesses. It allows them to see where they can make quick tactical adjustments for short term gains, as well as where to make long term strategic decisions, yielding healthier revenues and a higher level of customer service.
