DATA As a Diagnostic of Behavior

BestWork DATA is a powerful tool for diagnosing the cause of an individual’s

behavior. It accurately measures the hard-wired personality traits and cognitive

abilities of the individual, but what does it mean when an individual’s observed

behaviors do not match the DATA. In such cases, which are rare, DATA is playing

the same role as the basic blood test used by doctors as the first step in their

diagnostic process. The blood test looks for evidence of a bacterial infection. If it is positive, showing that the illness is bacterial, there are clear treatments the doctor can prescribe. If the blood test is negative, indicating that the infection is not bacterial, the doctor knows that there is another cause of the illness. In effect, the test directs the doctor to other solutions. DATA does the same thing. If a person is behaving differently than their DATA indicates, something is causing that aberration.

Assuming that the individual has the skills, education and experience needed

and that their attitude and values is acceptable, there are forces outside the

individual that are affecting their behavior. The business environment may be

stressful for a variety of reasons. It could be the health of the business, the

behavior of coworkers, changes occurring in the workplace or other things. It may also be caused by the individual’s personal situation outside of the business. This could be relationship issues, problems with children, other family issues, drugs or alcohol. In such cases, DATA is telling you to look elsewhere for the cause.
