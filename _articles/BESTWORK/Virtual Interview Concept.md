## Page 1

Virtual Interview™ Report Text

BestWork DATA™’s narrative report is written quite differently than the reports

common to most assessment instruments. First Generation instruments, Second

Generation instruments, and even some psychometrically advanced instruments

generate narratives that are written in a broad, generalized style that is designed to trigger a resonance with a fairly broad sample of the population. The information is often valuable, but it can often prove to be difficult and problematical for the assessment user or client to apply the descriptions to the job in question. This becomes even more difficult as the diversity of jobs increases while the information stays in its generalized form.

The extreme form of this is seen in very simple instruments which have narrative

reports resembling little more than horoscopes. This exploits a characteristic of human beings that is evidenced in what psychologists call the P.T. Barnum Effect. This refers to a commonly repeated experiment in which a simple assessment is administered to a group of people. Each of the participants is then given an identical personality report written in a broad resonance style.

Typically seventy percent or more of the people will describe the report as

accurate. The generalized wording stimulates some level of resonance across a

wide spectrum of participants despite the obvious diversity of the group.

BestWork DATA™uses a technique called virtual interview in which the text is

written specifically to integrate the described behaviors into the context of

specific job requirements. It is much as if the candidate was describing his

behavior in those circumstances. The interviewer would experience either a

positive or negative resonance with the described behavior and the job

requirements in question. This narrow band resonance provides the information

for effective decision making by the user.

The text is descriptive of the candidate’s personality traits and the behavioral

requirements of the job. The BestWork DATA™report does not deny any

candidate’s ability to perform any job. The report does describe the adaptations, stress, energy, or other costs that would be necessary to achieve the desired performance. This inclusionary style is valuable for the job candidate because it allows them to recognize both the Job Fit compatibilities and problems within a positive presentation.
