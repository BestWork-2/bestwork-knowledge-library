## Page 1

New Answers to Old Questions

In today’s digital world, it is hard to imagine being lost. We have GPS. Do

you have a common device that is not working? YouTube will have a

choice of videos to guide you through each step to fix it. On the road back

from Maine this summer, looking for a good place to eat, it was no

problem…Yelp had the answer. We found amazing cuisine on streets we

did not know, in towns we did not know .

In the business world, similar changes have happened: Hiring a salesperson…What if you knew who could sell and who could not? Who could sell products but not solutions?

Promoting an employee to management…What if you knew they

were enthusiastic, smart, motivated, organized, hard-working but

could not manage other people?

Strategic planning…What if you knew who thought tactically but

could not think strategically?

Struggling manager…What if you knew exactly what the problem

was and how to fix it?

How to collaborate & communicate…What if you knew exactly why

it was not happening and could see exactly how to fix it?

Performance is off…What if you could see exactly why that was

happening and how to fix it?

What if these things could happen without depending upon consultants?

What if you could see these things easily and without spending a lot of

money? What is the means to do this was always at hand with an array

tools to answer your questions?

of different applications.
