## Page 1

Better DATA = Better Decisions

A member of my tennis team recently told us about his son suffering a knee

injury while playing softball. The dad took his son to the family physician,

who promptly X-rayed the knee and examined him thoroughly. When he

was finished, he prescribed the classic rest-ice-compression-elevation,

assuring the son and the dad that the knee was simply sprained. As they

left the doctor’s office, the dad was not completely comfortable with the

situation, so he followed the recommendation of a friend to another

physician to get a second opinion. That doctor asked what had been done

so far. The dad said that his son’s knee had been examined and X-rayed

by the first doctor. The second doctor then explained that X-rays do not

show soft tissue but only bone. To get a clear picture of what has

happened, an MRI was necessary.

Once the MRI was finished, the doctor put it up on the screen. The dad ,

not a physician, looked at this incredibly sharp picture of the inside of his

son’s knee, and asked, pointing to a thin dark line, “What is this?” The

doctor replied, “That is where your son has a slight tear in the meniscus.”

With that information, a treatment plan became clear and all were satisfied.

The second physician was not smarter than the first one. The first doctor

did not miss anything in his examination. He simply lacked the DATA that

was available to the second physician. The power of DATA is that better

information automatically drives better decisions. Conversely, without better

DATA, it is impossible to make better decisions.

I am continually amazed that intelligent businesspeople persist in struggling

with people problems, such as bad hires, turnover, communication issues

and others, when the DATA to easily resolve them is readily available with

new technology. It requires no additional training or specialized knowledge.

In the same way as the MRI revealed the tear even to the untrained eye of

the father, DATA offers the same advantages to recruiters and managers.
