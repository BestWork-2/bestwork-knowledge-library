## Page 1

The 4th GPT

General-purpose technologies (GPT’s) are technologies that can affect an

entire economy. GPT’s have the potential to drastically alter businesses and

education through their impact on pre-existing practices and operational

beliefs. In the last fifty years, there have been only three GPT’s…personal

computers, the Internet and AI.

Agile Behavioral DATA is the fourth. Its ability to accurately and easily

measure the hard-wired personality traits and cognitive ability which are

foundational to anything involving people shatters the existing paradigms and

operational concepts, compelling a transformational rethink that offers

amazing and extraordinary opportunities for the world. The power of this

DATA is ignited and realized with the agility of the IT platform, which

translates the DATA into immediately accessible information that drives

action and awareness.

Connecting talent & jobs

The #1 challenge for businesses is finding and keeping talent. There are 12

million people actively seeking jobs each month, plus over 100 million with

jobs that want a different job. Unfortunately, the talent and the talent seekers

have no common language to accurately connect the capabilities of the talent

with the capabilities needed for the jobs. Resumés and job descriptions are

poor tools to manage life changing decisions. Behavioral DATA is a Rosetta

Stone for translating an individual’s hard-wired strengths and abilities into the job strengths needed to perform a certain job. Essentially bad hires do not happen and job seekers discover a career direction with a compass keyed to what they do well.

A Resilient Workforce

In a time of economic uncertainties, the concept of a resilient workforce is far

more than an interesting idea. It is a foundational necessity. Agile behavioral

DATA makes that possible. Winning sports teams are able to keep winning in

the face of injuries because they have the performance data for each member

of the team, and they can adjust, make substitutions, and adapt their strategy.

Their resilience comes from that knowledge. Behavioral DATA enables

businesses to adapt rapidly to changing economic factors.

Business Profitability & the Economic Health of the City

Experience with businesses has proven the impact on operational efficiency

and profitability when DATA is the operating system for the human

resources. Businesses have achieved 25% increases in productivity with even

simple applications, and with commensurate increases in net profits. When

this application of behavioral DATA is adopted by the businesses in a city, the

economic impact across the community is a game changer. The power of

behavioral DATA takes the promise of data analytics and transforms it into

reality…a reality in which education, entrepreneurship, workforce

development, economic strategies and the potential of people are forged into

meaningful action.
