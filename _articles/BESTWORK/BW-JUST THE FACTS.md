BestWork - Just the Facts

The value of BestWork is its ability to reveal the behavioral facts about

people…to tell what they can do and what they cannot do. Every human

behavior depends upon the hard-wired personality traits and cognitive

abilities of the individual. These are foundational, and effectively do not

change with training, coaching or incentives. BestWork measures these

accurately and reliably. Most importantly, it translates the DATA into specific

descriptions of the job behaviors that are critical to the successful

performance of the job. Simply put, it answers these kinds of questions: •Can this person persuade people to make buying decisions?

•Can this person work accurately with highly detailed information?

•Can this person direct the actions of others?

•Will this person be warm and friendly with customers?

•Can this person handle complex solution sales?

•Can this person handle routine tasks over an extended period of time?

•Can this person consistently follow the existing procedures?

The answers to these questions are facts that BestWork can easily provide.

They can be used for recruiting and understanding what positions best fit

job candidates. They can be used to solve performance problems. They

can be used to engineer more effective teams. They can be used for

workforce planning and strategic change programs. All of this is possible

without the need for special training or expert interpretations. The reports

can even be customized for special situations or circumstances.

Now the big question…if these facts are readily available, when do you

want to know them. Imagine instead of job performance, the question was

about parachutes and whether they were going to open. At what point

would you want to know that information? While you are still in the

plane…? Or after you jumped…? Do you want to know whether or not the
