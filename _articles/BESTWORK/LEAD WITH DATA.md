## Page 1

Lead with DATA

People are the critical variable in any business. Knowing their capabilities and

challenges is absolutely essential for any type of coaching or consulting. Generally this involves a series of interviews with the principals plus other supporting people.

No matter how skilled or experienced the interviewer, the result obtained is an

irregular mixture of subjective information, assorted observations and opinions

flavored with an array of emotions. In fact, the majority of time spent in most

consulting engagements is spent trying to parse that information into an actionable plan. Undoubtedly, talented professionals still managed to achieve some level of results, but those results were inevitably short-changed by the quality of the information.

That is no longer necessary. People have hard-wired personality traits and

cognitive abilities that are foundational to their thinking, learning and behaving.

Today, these can be easily measured, and that DATA can be used to describe

anyone’s job performance in any type of job. The DATA reveals how individuals

within a work group communicate, collaborate, interact and respond to any

situations. In effect, it is a blueprint that displays the operating system for human resources within any enterprise. Just as an MRI enables a doctor to examine soft tissue, BestWork enables consultants to see relationships and operational capabilities. They can see if the operational strengths of the company can support the operational strategies of the company. They can see exactly how to coach an executive, knowing their client’s issues straightaway, BestWork can direct the DATA to explore any aspect of job performance, such as critical thinking, collaboration or leadership.

The true power of behavioral DATA can only be realized at the outset of any

engagement. It creates an immediate and personal connection with the client that

transforms the coach or consultant into a trusted advisor, opening the doorway into a myriad of opportunities in that company.
