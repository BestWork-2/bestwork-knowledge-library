The Power of BestWork’s Unlimited Usage License

One of the most powerful advantages of BestWork is the annual unlimited

usage license. While it certainly reduces the cost per use, its most

important value is how it transforms the selection process. Once the license

has been purchased, there is effectively no incremental cost for using BW.

It is the most accurate and lowest cost element of the selection process

and as such, it moves to the front of the process. Clients typically ask every

applicant to complete BW as part of their process. This is easily done with

the customized landing page that comes with the BW license. This means

that only those candidates that BW shows CAN DO the job are passed on

to the next stage of the selection process. This eliminates the most

common cause of Bad Hires: hiring candidates who interview well but

perform the job poorly.

This is a time when a job posting for an executive assistant can produce

220 resumés and a posting for a project manager for a well known

company can produce over 400 resumés. Just reading that volume of

resumés is a prohibitive commitment of time. BW is the ideal way to screen

out the ones you do not need to read.

An incidental benefit of using BW with every applicant is that the resulting

DATA can be collected and analyzed to determine the effectiveness of the

company’s recruiting sources. This enable the company to fine tune those

efforts.

Another huge benefit of the unlimited license is the ability to assess all

existing employees. The BW reports can then be used to train and manage

those employees more effectively with an individual focus on each person’s

strengths and challenges. Upgrading to the management and team

engineering features of BestWork 8opens the door to limitless applications

to impact the development of the company’s human capital.
