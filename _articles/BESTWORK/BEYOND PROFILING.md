## Page 1

Beyond Profiling Top Performers

Simplifying Performance DATA for Recruiters & Managers

In the 1950’s, psychometric assessments were developed that accurately

and reliably measured the hard-wired traits and abilities of individuals.

BestWork uses an advanced reporting system based on Descriptive Job

Behaviors. Rather than look at how an individual matches an artificial

model, benchmark or profile of the top performers in a job, BestWork

describes how that individual would perform the Critical and Important job

behaviors needed in that job. Reports using benchmarking offer a

percentage match to the benchmark (Candidate A - 84%, Candidate B -

65%, and Candidate C - 78%). The obvious conclusion is that A is the best

choice with C being the next best. In most cases, B would be

unsatisfactory, having failed to meet the 70% threshold. It is difficult, if not

impossible to discern exactly what the differences are among the

candidates. How important is the missing % in each case. Unfortunately,

the text available with most products using this methodology is generalized

or even contradictory to such an extent that the users depend mainly on the

%. This allows strong matches that lack key factors to slip through, and

weaker matches who have the key factors but lack less important elements

to be excluded. Compounding the problem, the generalized text is of so

little use that the hiring decision is the last time the information on the

person’s performance is used.

BestWork describes the actual job behaviors of each individual, and the

information is provided in different forms. Simple charts are used to

compare multiple candidates. Job reports describe each behavioral

element of the job, identifying areas of strengths and areas of concern in

plain language. This allows for qualitative comparisons among candidates.

Different managers can also recognize the specific elements that are

important to them in their situations. The real value is that the DATA is

presented in many different forms, providing the users with information that

enhances the onboarding process, training programs, management,

coaching and other application throughout the life cycle of the employee.
