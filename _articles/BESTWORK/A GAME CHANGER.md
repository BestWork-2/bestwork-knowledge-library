complicated. The difficulty is best seen with a few comparisons to similar

different type of music media with infinitely greater features,

unimaginable in a world of CD’s.

Clayton Christensen explained it this way: Disruptive technologies change the value proposition in the market.

completely new context of value.

A Brief History of BestWork

In 1991, the idea that would eventually become BestWork was born. The

realization that all people had hard-wired personality traits and cognitive

abilities that could be measured was juxtaposed with my experience in

management consulting and training. That realization shattered the

paradigms of hiring, managing and training. It led me to write my first book,

Right Person - Right Job, Guess or Know. The challenge was that the

instruments that yielded good data were complex, and the ones that were

easy to use measured little of consequence.

In the late 1990’s, I engaged the former CEO of the American

Psychological Association and top psychometrician in North America to be

the technical advisor on a project to create a simple set of instruments that

not only produced solid data, but did not require interpretation. This led to a

completely new way of writing report text, which was termed virtual

interview.

A second version of an easy-to-use assessment was developed for a call

center specialty company. It again proved the value of simplicity and it

became the cornerstone of that company’s offerings.

BestWork was the third evolution of my original concept. It incorporated a

degree of flexibility and scale of application that had never been seen

before in the assessment industry. The original platform was built in 2009,

and four years of market testing followed, vetting the unique reporting

format that used individual job behaviors. Three years of product

development followed, both by design and by taking advantage of

opportunities afforded us as a highly entrepreneurial enterprise.

Our software platform is now in the third generation, being expanded to

include the features that our clients have requested. Major clients have

used the system for over 6 years, growing from early adopter special uses

to being fully integrated into their talent management system today. The

DATA is effectively the operating system for their human resources and

training, even prescribing the most appropriate training for managers based

on their individual DATA. They, with a few other clients, have been
