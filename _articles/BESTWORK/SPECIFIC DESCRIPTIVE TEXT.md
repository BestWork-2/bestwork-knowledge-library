Direct Integrated Performance Information (DIPI)

One of the fundamental operating principles of BestWork DATA is that each

DATA-based report must drive actionable decisions. For far too long, assessment

users have been satisfied with crude percentage matching methodologies.

Notwithstanding the occasional inaccuracies of those methods, once the

percentage score was used for a hiring decision, the reports offered little real

information on how to get the most from the new hire. Generalized reports,

regardless of the quality of the information, are of minimal value to most people.

They do not have the experience or the knowledge necessary to translate the

information into practical relevance for a particular job. All BestWork DATA

outputs are written for a specific audience and a specific decision point. It

describes in plain language how that individual will perform the critical job

behaviors in each job. This is called Direct Integrated Performance

Information or DIPI. BestWork DATA reports are easily understood without any

special training or expert consulting. There is text for almost any decision point in the lifecycle of an employee…selection, onboarding, training, career path, communication issues, team engineering, leadership, culture, management, performance problem solving, and more.

The whole DATA system was designed for this purpose. We have several

thousand “Lego blocks,” each one describing a single job behavior that is

supported by a single cognitive ability or personality trait, a prime driver. These can be combined to describe virtually any type of job. While this provides remarkable reports that offer specific information on any job at a decision-making level, we want to go beyond that. Where possible, we use the client’s terminology and job titles to create the reports. It is often a difference of only a few words.

However, when a company has disparate offices, a generalized list of reports

provokes numerous phone calls asking which report to use for what job. It

simplifies the customer’s experience if the job names on the reports are the same as the ones they use. This specific descriptive text is the hallmark of BestWork DATA reports, and it is the foundation for spreading the power of DATA throughout an enterprise.
